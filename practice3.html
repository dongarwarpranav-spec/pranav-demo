SankalpTaru Foundation

SankalpTaru Foundation is an IT-enabled NGO dedicated to restoring our planet’s green cover and uplifting communities across India. Aligned with our goals to afforestation, ecosystem restoration, water conservation, and community development, our operations reflect diverse ecosystems—from the snow-capped mountains of Ladakh to the arid deserts of Rajasthan, the drought-prone Vidarbha region, the Mahabalipuram coast, and the Sundarbans’ deltas. Our commitment to transparency ensures that every supporter, whether an individual or a multinational organisation, can witness the direct impact of their contribution. Each sapling is uniquely geotagged and recorded on the blockchain. Satellite imagery allows you to see these trees as green dots on the map, offering clear proof of your positive impact. Each tree you help plant stands as a powerful symbol of hope—an investment in our planet's future and the well-being of the communities we serve. Join us in our journey toward a cleaner, greener future—one tree at a time.
